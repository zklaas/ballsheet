## Download

| Your computer | Download | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** 10 or 11 | [BallSheet-Setup-Windows.exe](https://github.com/zklaas/ballsheet/releases/latest/download/BallSheet-Setup-Windows.exe) | Double-click it and follow the steps. BallSheet appears in the Start menu. |
| **macOS** 11 or newer (Apple silicon: M1 or later) | [BallSheet-macOS.dmg](https://github.com/zklaas/ballsheet/releases/latest/download/BallSheet-macOS.dmg) | Open it and drag **BallSheet** onto **Applications**. |
| **Linux** (64-bit PC) | [BallSheet-Linux-x86_64.AppImage](https://github.com/zklaas/ballsheet/releases/latest/download/BallSheet-Linux-x86_64.AppImage) | Right-click it > *Properties* > *Allow executing as program* (or `chmod +x` it), then double-click. |

Nothing else is needed: Python and everything BallSheet uses are inside.

### The first time you open it

These downloads are not signed with a paid developer certificate, so your computer
asks once before running them:

* **Windows** may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
* **macOS** may say BallSheet *"cannot be opened"* or *"could not be verified"*: click
  **Done**, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
  **Open Anyway** next to BallSheet, then open it again.
* **Linux** asks nothing, once the file is allowed to run.

If something doesn't work, please write to zklaastest-testing@yahoo.com.
